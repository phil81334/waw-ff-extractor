    About WaW FF Extractor
    
      
    
        
    
          An unofficial tool made for the World
          at War modding community.
        
    
        
    
          Extracts raw files, embedded WAV/XWMA sounds and CSV tables from PC
          World at War FFs. Inspired by Tom_Bmx’s FastFile Extractor, powered by
          OpenAssetTools.
        
    
        
    
          Raw files and embedded sounds keep their stored bytes. Tables keep
          their stored cells; CSV formatting is recreated. This is not a map
          decompiler or an asset reconstruction tool. Optional conversion adds ADPCM WAV copies while keeping the original XWMA files.
        
    
        
    
          Independent of Treyarch, Activision, UGX and Tom_Bmx. Open source
          under GPL-3.0. OpenAssetTools credits and source details are in
          ATTRIBUTION.md. WAV conversion uses FFmpeg (LGPL-2.1-or-later).
